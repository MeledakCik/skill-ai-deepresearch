### Synthesis decision

A **descriptive synthesis** of the two retrieved trials is defensible. Both compared SMS plus usual care with usual care. R01 reported an **8 percentage-point difference** in the proportion meeting a refill threshold at 12 weeks (95% CI 1 to 15; 240 participants). **No pooled effect is justified from the packet.** A threshold proportion and a mean scale change estimate different quantities, at different follow-up times. Neither report supplies the other outcome or supports conversion to a common effect. For the completed review, independently verify extraction and assess risk of bias for each outcome. Pool only within groups with a comparable adherence definition, effect measure and follow-up window. Otherwise, retain study-level estimates and confidence intervals.
