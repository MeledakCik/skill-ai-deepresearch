The draft’s nine-point claim compares that pass rate with the 2024 population estimate. Only the two survey estimates support a year-to-year comparison, and their difference is **2 percentage points**. (`draft-headline.md`, §1; `statistics-report.md`, §3; `education-report.md`, §4)

**What remains unresolved:** The survey’s 95% intervals overlap (79–85% in 2024 and 81–87% in 2025).
