### Protocol addendum dated 22 November 2026

This addendum applies prospectively to the remaining work. The last documented review status is 1 March 2026. Retain Q1 and Q2 as preliminary searches, preserving their original dates, exact queries or procedures, filters, counts and record IDs. Time-stamp this addendum before running another search. Date and justify subsequent amendments.

1. **Apply the board’s eligibility criteria.** Include randomized trials published from 1 January 2018 through 31 December 2025 in English or Indonesian. Participants must be adults aged 18 or older with diagnosed hypertension managed in primary care. Compare SMS medication-adherence reminders alone or added to usual care with usual care or no SMS reminder. Require a validated adherence measure or pharmacy-refill adherence at eight weeks or later. Mixed-condition studies qualify only when hypertension results are reported separately. (`review-brief.md`)
2. **Complete the search.** Search the full eligible publication window in MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL and the Indonesian GARUDA index. Combine hypertension and SMS reminder concepts using subject headings where supported and English and Indonesian text terms. Allow retrieval of reports whose titles or abstracts omit adherence or randomization terminology. Search ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, relevant conference proceedings and thesis repositories for leads to eligible reports. Check references and forward citations for each included trial. Refresh database searches before sign-off to capture late indexing while retaining the publication cutoff.
3. **Make each search reproducible.** Log the searcher, date, source and platform, coverage, exact query as executed, fields, filters, result count and export filename. For citation checks, record the seed report, direction and lookup method. For manually searched sources, record the pages or issues examined. Retain raw exports, assign stable record IDs, preserve every source-to-record link and log duplicate matches and their basis. Link multiple reports of one trial under a study ID.
4. **Verify selection and resolve access.** Have two reviewers independently screen all unique records at both applicable stages, including a fresh assessment of the preliminary decisions. Verify the R04 duplicate link. Record each reviewer’s decision, one primary reason per exclusion, retrieval attempts and dates, and how disagreements were resolved. A preliminary narrative synthesis is defensible now. R01 reported an **8 percentage-point difference** in the proportion meeting a refill threshold at 12 weeks (95% CI 1 to 15).
