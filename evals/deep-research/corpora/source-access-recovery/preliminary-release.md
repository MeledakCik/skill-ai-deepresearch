# Preliminary agency release

Publisher: City Transport Agency. Published 20 January 2026.

## §1 Provisional dashboard result

The dashboard reported a provisional “execution rate” of 72%, calculated as Rp68.4 billion in recorded payments divided by a preliminary Rp95 billion allocation. The release says the ledger and allocation remain subject to reconciliation and that a final dataset will follow. This release reports the same provisional dashboard; it is not a separate measurement.
