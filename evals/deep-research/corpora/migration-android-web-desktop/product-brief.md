# Product brief (fictional)

## §1 Request

FieldLoop is an Android maintenance app used by 70 field technicians and 12 dispatchers. Technicians need to inspect equipment in locations with no reliable network for up to 48 hours. Dispatchers now request a browser-based scheduling and assignment console. Two supervisors ask whether a macOS desktop client would be better. Their office computers are managed and use Chrome or Safari; they have not identified a macOS-only capability.

The sponsor proposes copying the Android database to the browser, migrating all 11 screens, and shipping in six weeks. Those are proposals, not verified technical facts. Field technicians must keep their current Android workflow during the dispatcher-client rollout.

## §2 Decision requested

Recommend which user workflow belongs on which target, what should remain on Android, and the smallest feasibility check before estimating delivery. Do not write code or decommission the current client.
