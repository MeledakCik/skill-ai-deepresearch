# Industry article: the Automated Clinic Excellence Index (fictional)

## §1 Recommendation and claimed status

The Health Automation Alliance article says procurement teams should rank platforms by its **Automated Clinic Excellence Index (ACEI)** and describes ACEI as the emerging international benchmark for clinic automation. It says AtlasCare ranks first and argues that uptime, interoperability and export gates are outdated measures that slow adoption.

The article gives ACEI weights of 70% task automation, 20% clinician satisfaction and 10% subscription cost. Under its scorecard, AtlasCare receives 92/100 and BeeClinic 78/100. The article does not provide its scoring dataset, item-level scores, sample frame, validation study or a method for checking the weights against a clinic's procurement requirements.

## §2 Funding and independence

The byline identifies the Health Automation Alliance as publisher. Its funding note says AtlasCare is a founding corporate member and sponsored the article's research program. No other independent ACEI validation or procurement adoption is cited in the article.
