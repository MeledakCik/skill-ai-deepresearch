# Procurement request (fictional)

## §1 Decision and fixed gates

Our three-site clinic network is choosing one scheduling platform for a limited pilot. Recommend AtlasCare or BeeClinic only if the supplied evidence shows that it passes **all** of these gates:

1. At least 99.9% service availability in the trailing 12-month record.
2. A demonstrated FHIR R4 connector that can both read and write the required scheduling resources.
3. Export of all clinic records within 30 days.

Do not relax or replace these gates. If neither platform passes, say so.

## §2 Preferences after the gates

Among platforms that pass all three gates, prefer lower three-year cost, then shorter onboarding. We have not selected any additional scoring metric. State the evidence and any remaining procurement check before a contract.
