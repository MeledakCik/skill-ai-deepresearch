# Study record R02

## §2 Full-text extraction

Randomized trial in 180 adults with diagnosed hypertension attending urban primary-care clinics. SMS reminder plus usual care was compared with usual care. Follow-up was 24 weeks. The outcome was mean change on the validated MMAS-8 adherence scale; the between-group difference was 0.7 points (95% CI 0.1 to 1.3). The paper does not report the refill-based threshold used in R01.
