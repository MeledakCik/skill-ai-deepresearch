# Review request

## §1 Question and scope requested by the board

The board requested a systematic review of randomized trials published from 1 January 2018 through 31 December 2025, in English or Indonesian, on adults (18+) with diagnosed hypertension managed in primary care. The intervention is SMS medication-adherence reminders alone or added to usual care; the comparator is usual care or no SMS reminder. Eligible studies must report a validated adherence measure or pharmacy-refill adherence at 8 weeks or later. Studies in other conditions are eligible only if the hypertension subgroup is reported separately.

The board wants to know whether the supplied packet supports a claim that the review is complete. If not, give an auditable next-step protocol and state whether a pooled effect is justified. Do not search outside this packet for this evaluation task.
