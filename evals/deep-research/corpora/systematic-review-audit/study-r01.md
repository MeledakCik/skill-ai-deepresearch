# Study record R01

## §2 Full-text extraction

Randomized trial in 240 adults with diagnosed hypertension attending rural primary-care clinics. SMS reminder plus usual care was compared with usual care. Follow-up was 12 weeks. The reported outcome was the proportion meeting the study's refill-based adherence threshold; the between-group difference was 8 percentage points (95% CI 1 to 15). The trial did not use the self-report scale reported by R02.
