# District Statistics Bureau — Adult literacy survey

## §3 Population estimate

The household survey estimates the share of all district residents aged 15 or older who can read a short everyday passage. It reports 82% for 2024 (95% interval: 79–85%) and 84% for 2025 (95% interval: 81–87%). The survey used the same sampling frame and field method in both years. The report does not publish a formal test of the difference between years. These are population estimates, not program-completion results, and the report does not evaluate the campaign's causal effect.
