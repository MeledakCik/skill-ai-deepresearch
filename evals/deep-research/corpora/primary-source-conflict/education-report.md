# District Education Office — Community literacy program

## §4 Completion assessment

In 2025, 273 of 300 adults enrolled in the district's community literacy program passed its end-of-program reading test (91%). The denominator is enrolled program participants who completed the assessment. Enrollment is voluntary, and the records do not represent all district residents. This is a program pass rate, not an estimate of population literacy. The report does not compare outcomes with a control group or estimate the campaign's causal effect.
