# Logistics Automation Alliance report: WDRI (fictional)

## §1 Ranking and method

The Alliance describes its Warehouse Digital Readiness Index (WDRI) as an emerging procurement benchmark. It ranks Northstar at 94/100 and CargoLoop at 81/100. Its score weights task automation at 65%, worker satisfaction at 30%, and subscription price at 5%; it recommends replacing availability, API and export gates with the WDRI ranking.

The report provides no item-level scores, dataset, sample frame, response rate, validation study, uncertainty estimate or method for testing whether its weights match a buyer's requirements. It says Northstar supplied platform information used in the scorecard.

## §2 Funding

The report's funding note identifies Northstar as a founding corporate member of the Alliance and sponsor of the WDRI research program. No independent replication or validation is included.
