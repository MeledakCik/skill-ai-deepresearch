# Procurement request (fictional)

## §1 Decision and fixed gates

Our three-clinic network is selecting an inventory and warehouse platform for a limited deployment. Recommend Northstar or CargoLoop only if the supplied evidence shows that it passes **all** of these gates:

1. At least 99.9% service availability in the trailing 12-month record.
2. A demonstrated WMS API v2 integration that can both read and write the required inventory and order resources.
3. Full export of inventory, orders and audit records within seven calendar days.

Do not relax or replace these gates. If neither platform passes, say so.

## §2 Preferences after the gates

Among platforms that pass all three gates, prefer lower three-year total cost, then shorter onboarding. We have not selected an additional score or ranking metric.
