# Northstar market brief: “Three independent reports back WDRI” (fictional)

## §1 Claim

Northstar says WDRI has been “independently validated across the market” and presents three badges: the Alliance WDRI report, TradeTech Review, and the MarketOps Advisory brief. All three display Northstar at 94 and CargoLoop at 81.

## §2 Linked sources

The Alliance report is the source of the WDRI scores. TradeTech says it reproduced the Alliance table. MarketOps says its table uses the Alliance scores and cites TradeTech among its references. Northstar supplies no separate study, underlying data, buyer survey or replication.
