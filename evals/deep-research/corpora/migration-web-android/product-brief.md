# Product brief (fictional)

## §1 Request

StokArah is used by 12 small warehouses and 36 receiving staff. The sponsor asks for an Android client because warehouse Wi-Fi is unreliable. Staff should scan items and record goods received while offline, then sync when connectivity returns. The existing office web client must continue working. The managed device fleet runs Android 10 or later; distribution is planned through the company's managed Play account.

The sponsor says the product has nine screens, so the Android migration should take two weeks. They expect the current API to be reusable and assume saving receipts on the device is enough to provide offline support. These are sponsor estimates and assumptions, not engineering findings.

## §2 Decision requested

Recommend a feasible first migration slice and identify what must be verified before committing to a delivery date. Do not implement the change or approve a release.
