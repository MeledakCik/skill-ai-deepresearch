# Workflow observations (fictional)

## §1 Receiving flow

Staff sign in, select a delivery, scan or enter a SKU, enter quantity, and submit the receipt. The browser form currently needs a network connection at submission. A reload clears the in-progress form according to the supplied support note. No device-camera scanning test or Android user study is supplied.

## §2 Connectivity sample

In a convenience sample of 40 receiving shifts across three warehouses, 14 shift reports mention a network interruption. Eight reports mention re-entering a receipt after the web form displayed a save error. The sample is not a randomized study and does not establish the frequency of lost or duplicate server records.

The packet includes no measured sync backlog, duplicate-write count, task completion baseline, app startup target, accessibility check, or security review for local storage. These are open measurements, not zeros.
