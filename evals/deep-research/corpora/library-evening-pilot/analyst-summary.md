# Pilot analysis (fictional)

## §1 Observed count

The ten-evening schedule produced usable counts for **9 evenings**. The register contains **63 visits across 13 staffed hours**, or **4.846 visits per observed staffed hour** (rounded to 4.85). This calculation excludes S10 because its visit count is unavailable.

## §2 Missing interval

S10 had 2 scheduled staffed hours. The telemetry system reported an outage; no manual tally is available. Total scheduled time was 15 hours, of which 13 hours had usable visit counts. The actual S10 visit count is unknown.

## §3 Study design

This is a small pilot without a control group, randomized schedule or comparable pre-pilot evening series. The observations describe the pilot; they do not identify the causal effect of adding the hour or establish demand on future weeks.
