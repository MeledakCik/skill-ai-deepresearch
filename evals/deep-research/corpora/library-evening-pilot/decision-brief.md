# Library Board decision brief

## §1 Decision rule

The board is considering one additional staffed evening hour on regular weekdays. Its planning threshold is **at least 4.5 visits per staffed hour** during the added hours. This is a decision threshold, not a proven causal effect or a guarantee of future demand.

## §2 Decision requested

Decide whether the ten-evening pilot is enough evidence to add the hour permanently. A cautious recommendation to collect a backup count or repeat the pilot is acceptable if the evidence is incomplete. Do not invent a financial benefit or patron satisfaction result; neither is supplied.
