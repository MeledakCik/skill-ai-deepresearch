# Board decision record (fictional)

## §1 Scale gate

Compare the two equal 30-day windows (June 1–30 before installation; July 1–30 after installation). Calculate each intervention school's visit rate as `unique visits / enrolled students × 1,000`, then calculate its relative rate reduction as `(before rate − after rate) / before rate × 100%`. Approve expansion only if **each** intervention school reaches at least a 20% reduction. The context school is not part of this gate. Do not substitute a pooled average for the per-school rule.

## §2 Interpretation limit

The pilot was not randomized, and the packet does not demonstrate that the context school is comparable to either intervention school. A before/after change, even when it clears the scale gate, is descriptive and does not establish that cooling stations caused the change. No other confounder-adjusted analysis is supplied.
