## §2 Measurement definitions

The chart counts unique nurse visits classified as heat-related in each 30-day window. Each student may contribute at most one visit per day. Enrollment counts above are the required denominator for the board's rate: visits per 1,000 enrolled students. No student-day denominator, adjustment for attendance, or randomized assignment is supplied. The chart's bars, not this registry, contain the visit counts.
